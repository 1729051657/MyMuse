MyMuse · 数据说明

本服务由部署方运营，基于 nanoMuse 构建，与 nanoMuse 上游运营的公共服务及 Meta 无关联。

账号服务保存加密的邮箱、登录会话、设备、用量和必要的网络访问记录，用于登录、设备互联、额度计算和故障排查。每个账号的云端助手将对话、文件、记忆、任务和连接器配置保存在独立的数据卷中。服务器运营者拥有基础设施管理权限。

模型请求会发送至本服务或你自己配置的模型服务商。对话同步开关控制多设备之间的对话副本，数据共享偏好可在「数据控制」中管理。默认部署关闭用于训练的数据共享与账号服务的完整模型请求录制；助手仍会保存自己的聊天和工作记录。

你可以在助手中删除文件、聊天和记忆，并在「账号」中使用已有的账号管理功能。删除账号的云端助手数据卷和运营备份需要联系本服务运营者；账号删除不会自动清除这些副本。保留周期、运营者联系方式及适用的服务条款由部署方在开放注册前补充说明。

Data information

This deployment is operated by its service operator, built on nanoMuse, and is independent of the upstream public service and Meta.

The account service stores encrypted e-mail addresses, sessions, devices, usage and necessary network access records for sign-in, device connections, metering and troubleshooting. Each cloud assistant stores chats, files, memories, tasks and connector configuration in separate volumes. The server operator has infrastructure access.

Model requests go to the model provider configured by the service or by you. Conversation sync controls cross-device chat copies. Manage data-sharing preferences under Data controls. This deployment disables training-data sharing and full model-request recording in the account service by default. The assistant still saves its own conversations and work records.

Delete files, chats and memories in the assistant and use the existing account controls under Account. Contact the operator to remove assistant volumes and operator backups; deleting an account does not automatically remove these copies. The operator must provide retention periods, contact details and applicable service terms before opening registration.
